# Approach

I make things from zero — pre-product diagnostics, weird internal tools, things that exist on
paper before they exist in software.

The work on this site is mostly process: hypothesis canvases, flowcharts, business models, and
the apps and code that come out the other side. Process is what's usually missing from a
portfolio, and it's the only thing that's actually repeatable.

## How I work

**On paper first.** Anything that's going to live in software exists on paper for a few weeks
beforehand. Most of what's wrong with a product is wrong on paper too — it's just cheaper to
catch there.

**Hypothesis-shaped.** Every project I take starts with a written belief, a written reason, and
a written signal that would prove me wrong. If I can't write the signal, I'm not ready to build.

**Append-only.** I keep a log of what I believed and when. Beliefs without dates can't be
falsified. Most product post-mortems fail because nobody wrote down what they thought *before*
the launch.

**Small wedges.** I'd rather ship a one-feature tool that thirty operators love than a ten-feature
tool that three hundred kind-of-use.

## What I'm looking for

Early-stage product work where the problem is still slippery. Diagnostics. Internal tools.
Pre-PMF research and prototyping. Not great at series B optimization. Very good at the messy
first six months.
