# Approach

I make things from zero. Pre-product research. Hypothesis-driven prototypes. Whole ventures, sometimes. The work on this site is the process: canvases, flowcharts, voice agents, the apps and decks that came out the other side.

## Why

Understanding people is the whole job. Every product I have built started with thirty interviews. Every interview I have run started with a question I was not sure I was allowed to ask.

## How

I got the bug for business at sixteen. Law school taught me to think in systems. Travel taught me the parts of business that no school covers. Teaching gave me the tools to coach, manage and align teams under pressure.

I now use product thinking and human-centred design to influence strategies, ship products and turn early ideas into things that have a P&L.

## What I am looking for

A fast-moving team working on something high-impact and people-shaped. Something where the problem is still slippery, where research will change the answer, and where the team is ready to be wrong in public.

I am good at the messy first six months. Less good at series-B optimisation.
