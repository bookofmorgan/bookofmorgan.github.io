## Problem

Every product team I've talked to has the same private folder: screenshots of customer messages,
half-finished interview notes, Slack DMs forwarded to themselves. The signal is there. It just
never makes it into the decision.

## Hypothesis

<Hypothesis
  belief="Teams will route customer signal through a single append-only inbox if writing to it takes under five seconds"
  reason="the friction isn't reading the signal, it's getting it into a place where it's read"
  signal="60% of captured signal is referenced in a roadmap discussion within 30 days"
/>

## Process

Built a CLI first. `signal log "user X said Y"` from anywhere. Then a tiny web UI for browsing.
Then a weekly digest that surfaces patterns. The order mattered — every other tool I'd seen
started with the UI and added the capture loop later, and capture is where these tools die.

## Outcome

<Stat value="< 4s" label="median time from thought to logged signal" />
<Stat value="68%" label="of signal referenced in a roadmap discussion within 30 days" />

Beat the hypothesis on both metrics. The CLI is what makes it work. Browser-based capture loops
never beat a one-line shell command.

## Learnings

- **Capture friction is the whole game.** Storage, browsing, search, summarization — none of it
  matters if the capture step has any friction at all.
- **Append-only is a feature.** Operators wanted to *not* edit past entries. Wrong-but-recorded
  is more useful than right-but-revised.
